### 4.1 Hero — keep the headline, add the Cause + one CTA
Headline stays: *"I build the frameworks that become the standard before there's a market."*

### 4.2 Manifesto / "What I believe" (new `/manifesto` or a homepage band) — Title of Liberty
Drawn from your stated positions (AI-first; anti-causal-reductionism; systems over formulas; build-before-the-market; patient failed by medicine):
> **I believe the standard arrives late, and someone has to build it early.**
> I believe most of what we call "chronic and irreversible" is unsolved, not unsolvable.
> I believe causal reductionism — one cause, one cure — is the most expensive mistake in medicine.
> I believe if a job can be done by AI, it should be, including mine.
> I believe you don't need permission, a PhD, or a market that already exists to be right.
> I build frameworks for the problems the institutions haven't named yet. Heavy metals in food are next.

**Enemy (us-vs-them) = causal reductionism, credentialism, and institutions that move too slowly — never a named person or brand** (legal + the HMI firewall).

### 4.3 Self-identification — name the audience (pick one, or propose your own)
The people who follow you think in systems, distrust single-cause stories, and act before consensus. Candidate identities: **"Systems thinkers,"** **"Early builders,"** **"the people who were right early."** Use it in the manifesto and newsletter ("for people who'd rather be right early than agree late").

### 4.4 Value ladder — add the free rung
Make `/karens-brain` (or a newsletter) the explicit **free** top-of-ladder, then Advisory → Roundtable → Board. One framing line:
> *Free: read how I think. → $1,500/hr: ask me your specific question. → $75K+: bring me your hardest problem. → Board/advisory: build with me.*
